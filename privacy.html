Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 (anonymous statistics added)

Fine Print: The Exit Clause (“the app”) is made by Hadi Samara. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

The app does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. To improve the levels, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics (for example “level 3-02 was quit after 14 deaths”), which you can turn off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous stats.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

When the setting is on, the app sends these events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU:

  • Leaving a level: the level number, whether it was cleared, how many deaths and restarts, the time spent, and where in the level and how each death happened
  • Starting the app, the first launch after installing, finishing the game, claiming a daily reward, and unlocking a cosmetic with paperclips
  • Basic technical information: app version, iOS version, device model and language

Each install is identified only by a random number created on your device and scrambled (hashed) before it is sent. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID, advertising identifier, contacts, location or anything else about you, it is not used to track you across apps or websites, and it is never sold or shared for advertising. The statistics are used only to find levels that are too hard, too easy or broken.

Turning the setting off stops all sending immediately and deletes any statistics that were waiting to be sent.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, deaths, paperclips, unlocked cosmetics and settings) is saved in a file on your device so you can continue where you left off. Apart from the anonymous statistics above, it never leaves your device, except as part of your own iCloud or device backups, which Apple manages. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The anonymous statistics described above contain no personal information.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated too.
