Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Fine Print: The Exit Clause (“the app”) is made by Hadi Samara. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

The app does not collect any personal data, store it on a server, share it or sell it. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, deaths, paperclips, unlocked cosmetics and settings) is saved in a file on your device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device, except as part of your own iCloud or device backups, which Apple manages. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated too.
