Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want to complain to the Ministry of Exits? Email hadi.sam1998@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number (it’s on the form in the top-left corner).

Frequently asked questions

Is every level actually beatable?

Yes. Every level is tested automatically before each release. The obvious way is usually wrong; read the fine print under the Rule Card and watch what kills you.

The controls suddenly went backwards.

That’s a level, not a bug. The arrows on the buttons flip when the controls do.

Where is the ACTION button?

It only appears in levels that need it, in the bottom-right next to jump.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone and survives updates. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings (the gear on the title screen) → Reset progress.

Are there ads or purchases?

No. Paperclips are earned by playing and only unlock cosmetics.

The game is too hard.

That’s the paperwork talking. Every death is filed in your Case File, and the worst offenders earn hats.
